Listen to ChatGPT & Claude answers on your Mac

Long AI replies are easier to take in by ear. Here's how to listen — including inside desktop apps where selection or built-in voice features may not cover your workflow.

The short version

ChatGPT and Claude have built-in voice or read-aloud features in supported surfaces, but availability changes by app, plan, region, and rollout, and those features stay inside their own products. The free Mac built-in (Speak Selection) depends on selectable text, which can be awkward in some desktop apps.

The reliable Mac workflow is a tool that reads your clipboard: copy the answer, paste it into Sorrel, and it speaks. Sorrel does exactly that, strips the markdown and code so it sounds clean, and — with macOS system voices — processes the reading text on your Mac. The optional hotkey is there if you prefer it.

The reliable way

Listen to copied answers in 3 steps

1

Copy the answer

In the Claude or ChatGPT app — or your browser — select the reply and press ⌘C. Because Sorrel reads the clipboard, this works even where selection is awkward.

2

Replace & Play

Open Sorrel and click Replace & Play. Sorrel strips the markdown, code and links, then reads the answer aloud with macOS system voices.

3

Listen, privately

With macOS system voices, playback happens on your Mac. No Sorrel account is required for reading, and reading text is not uploaded to Sorrel.

Why this helps: it works anywhere you can copy text, it cleans up the formatting so a long answer sounds like a person, and with macOS system voices, reading text is processed on your Mac.
FAQ

Common questions

Can ChatGPT read its answers aloud?

ChatGPT has voice and read-aloud features in supported ChatGPT surfaces, but availability can vary by app, plan, region, and rollout. Those features stay inside ChatGPT; Sorrel is for copied text from any app.

Can Claude read its answers aloud?

Claude has voice features in supported Claude surfaces, with availability changing by app and rollout. To listen to copied Claude text on your Mac, Sorrel reads your clipboard — copy the answer, paste it into Sorrel, and it speaks.

Why doesn't the built-in Mac read-aloud work in the ChatGPT or Claude apps?

The built-in macOS Speak Selection workflow depends on selectable text. If selection is awkward or unavailable in a desktop app, Sorrel can read whatever you copy instead.

How do I listen to AI answers privately?

Use system-voice playback. With Sorrel's macOS system-voice path, audio is generated on your Mac and reading text is not uploaded to Sorrel. Optional cloud voices send filtered text to the provider you choose.
