Listen to ChatGPT & Claude answers on your Mac

Long AI replies are easier to take in by ear. Here's how to listen — including inside the desktop apps, where the usual tricks fail.

The short version

ChatGPT can read its replies aloud, but only inside ChatGPT and in the cloud. Claude reads replies aloud too — but only in its web and phone apps (Voice Mode), not the Mac desktop app. And the free Mac built-in (Speak Selection) often won't work inside the Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps, because it needs to select the text first.

The reliable way is a tool that reads your clipboard: copy the answer, press a hotkey, and it speaks — in any app. Sorrel does exactly that, strips the markdown and code so it sounds clean, and — with your Mac's built-in voices — keeps everything on your Mac.

FAQ

Common questions

Can ChatGPT read its answers aloud?

Yes — ChatGPT has a read-aloud button for its replies. The catch: it only works inside ChatGPT, in the cloud. It won't read a Claude answer, another app, or anything else on your Mac.

Can Claude read its answers aloud?

Claude can read replies aloud in its web and phone apps (Voice Mode), but not in the Mac desktop app. To listen on your Mac's desktop you need a separate tool. Sorrel does it by reading your clipboard — copy the answer, press a hotkey, and it speaks.

Why doesn't the built-in Mac read-aloud work in the ChatGPT or Claude apps?

Those desktop apps handle text in a way where macOS Speak Selection and text selection often don't work. Sorrel reads whatever you copy instead, so it works in those apps.

How do I listen to AI answers privately?

Use a tool that speaks on-device. Sorrel generates audio with your Mac's own voices, so the text never leaves your computer — unlike cloud read-aloud features that send it to a server.
