Why Sorrel

Cleaner than raw Speak Selection. Simpler than a reading platform.

Use Sorrel when you want

  • A clean paste-and-play reader for copied text.
  • Markdown, links, citations, and footnotes stripped before audio.
  • System Voice playback without an account or subscription.
  • A pay-once Mac utility instead of a reading platform.

Use another tool when you need

  • OCR for scanned PDFs or images.

  • Cross-device libraries, mobile apps, or audiobook workflows.
  • Commercial voiceover export and production controls.
Privacy

Local by default, with clear limits.

With System Voice playback, the reading text stays on your Mac. Sorrel reads the clipboard only when you paste, click Replace & Play, press the optional hotkey, or invoke a command.

Cloud voices are optional. If you choose one, the filtered text is sent to that provider over HTTPS using your own key. Purchase and license delivery use Gumroad and Sorrel's license service.

FAQ

Reading questions.

Can Sorrel read PDFs?

Yes, when you can copy/select the PDF text. Scanned PDFs, images, DRM pages, or copy-protected files need OCR or another tool first.

Is Sorrel for dyslexia or ADHD?

Some people use audio when reading is tiring, slow, or hard to stick with. Sorrel does not diagnose, treat, or make medical claims.

Can I follow along visually?

Yes. Sorrel highlights the current sentence while reading so you can listen and follow the text.
